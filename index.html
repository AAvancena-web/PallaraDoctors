Our Services

Comprehensive Care, Tailored For You

One clinic for your family's everyday health needs — check-ups, chronic care, procedures and preventative health, all under one roof in Pallara.

General Consultations

Everyday illnesses, health concerns, medical certificates and referrals with a GP who knows your history.

Child Health & Immunisations

Developmental checks, childhood vaccinations and gentle care for common paediatric illnesses.

Women's Health

Family planning, Implanon and Mirena, cervical screening, antenatal support and menopause management.

Skin Checks & Excisions

Full skin examinations, mole checks and on-site removal of suspicious lesions.

Chronic Disease Management

Structured care plans for diabetes, asthma, hypertension and heart disease, reviewed regularly.

Minor Surgery & Procedures

Cysts, ingrown toenails, wound suturing, dressings and iron infusions performed in-clinic.

Driver & WorkCover Medicals

Commercial, taxi and rideshare licence assessments, WorkCover claims and pre-employment checks.

Referrals, X-Rays & Pathology

Fast referrals to trusted local imaging and labs, with results reviewed by your own GP.

Every Stage of Life

Care That Grows With You

For our youngest patients we offer immunisations, developmental checks and gentle care for common childhood illnesses. As families grow, we support adolescent health, adult screening and preventative advice.

Women's health is a core strength — from contraception and antenatal care through to cervical screening and menopause management. Men's health is equally prioritised with preventative screening and lifestyle support.

Chronic & Preventative Care

Lifelong Health Management

Our GPs create structured care plans for diabetes, asthma, hypertension and heart disease — with regular monitoring, medication reviews and coordinated care alongside your specialists.

We also provide comprehensive health assessments for older adults and support for mental health and emotional wellbeing at every age.
